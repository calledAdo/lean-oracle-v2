---
title: How a price is made
description: How each publisher turns exchange order books into one price, confidence and moving average.
---

Every publisher computes each feed independently, with the same rules and settings, once per tick.
The committee then signs one result per feed. The settings are part of a versioned
[committee config](/docs/operate/governance), and every signed update carries the hash of the config
that produced it.

## 1. Which exchanges count

A feed lists the exchanges (venues) allowed to price it, and each must trade exactly that pair. At
each tick, an exchange counts only if:

- its connection delivered something recently (liveness, not price movement: a quiet market on a
  live connection still counts);
- its order book is not crossed and its spread is within a limit;
- the best bid and ask each have real size behind them (the dust filter).

## 2. One price per exchange

For most feeds, the exchange's price is the median of its mid price, sampled every 100 ms through a
one-second window. CKB pairs, which trade more thinly, use a 60-second trade VWAP where there were
trades.

## 3. One price per feed

- Take the **median** across exchanges. For an even count, it is the floor of the mean of the middle
  two.
- Drop exchanges further than the allowed deviation from that median, and take the median again.
- At least a minimum number of exchanges must remain, or the feed is skipped for that tick.
- **Confidence** is the larger of the median half-spread and the median absolute deviation between
  exchanges.
- **Source time** is the median time of the exchange data used.

## 4. Moving average

`ema_price` and `ema_conf` use a one-hour half-life. They are computed from the committee's signed
history in integer arithmetic, so every publisher gets the same value.

## Launch settings

| Setting | majors | USDT/USD | ckb |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tick | 1 s | 1 s | 2 s |
| Exchange price | mid, 1 s | mid, 1 s | 60 s VWAP, else mid |
| Liveness | 2 s | 2 s | 60 s |
| Max spread | 0.5% | 0.1% | 3% |
| Max deviation | 1% | 0.3% | 3% |
| Minimum exchanges | /USD 2 of 3, /USDT 3 of 7, /USDC 3 of 8 | 2 of 3 | /USDT 3 of 5, /USDC 2 of 3 |

## Rules every feed follows

- **Native pairs only.** No currency is ever converted. USDC/USD is not offered because only two
  exchanges trade it directly.
- **At least two exchanges must agree**, so no single exchange can set a price.
- **One spare market.** A feed lists at least one more market than it needs, so one exchange going
  down does not stop it.
