title: Introduction
description: What Lean Oracle is, who it is for and what you get.
---

Lean Oracle delivers exchange prices to applications on [Nervos CKB](https://www.nervos.org/). It is a
**pull oracle**. Prices are signed off-chain every second, and your application brings one on chain
in the same transaction that uses it.

## What you get

- **Prices from leading exchanges.** Each feed is priced from the exchanges that trade that exact
  pair, such as Binance, Coinbase, Kraken and OKX. A price comes with a confidence interval and a
  moving average.
- **A new price every second.** Updates are signed every second for major assets and every two
  seconds for CKB.
- **Prices your contract can verify.** Every update carries the signatures of a committee of
  publishers. The on-chain script checks them before your feed cell accepts a price.
- **A cell you own.** You create your own feed cell and decide who may update it. There are no shared
  cells to compete for.
- **Cheap reads.** Once your feed cell holds a verified price, a contract reads it for about 38,000
  cycles.

## Who it is for

- **Application developers** who need a price in a transaction: lending, derivatives, prediction
  markets, payments and limit orders. The [TypeScript SDK](/docs/reference/sdk) fetches updates and
  builds the transactions.
- **Contract developers** who write CKB scripts in Rust. The [`lean-oracle-common`](/docs/reference/rust)
  crate reads and checks a feed cell.
