title: Publishers

A **publisher** is an operator that watches the exchanges, computes prices and signs them. Publishers
are permissioned: each one's public key is written into a committee cell on CKB, and only those keys
count.

## What a publisher runs

Each publisher runs the publisher service, a Docker image built from the open-source repository. In
every tick it:

1. **Records** live order books from every exchange its committees use, over WebSockets (REST for
   MEXC).
2. **Observes**: computes each feed's price with the committee's rules and signs that observation.
3. **Shares** its observation with the other publishers over authenticated connections.
4. **Proposes or checks**: one publisher, rotating each tick, proposes the tick's update; the others
   check it against their own observations within a tolerance.
5. **Signs** the update's header once a quorum agrees, and serves the finalized update to mirrors.

Each publisher keeps a local database that refuses to sign two different updates for the same tick,
even after a restart.

## Why several publishers

A single publisher is a single point of failure and of trust. With `n` publishers and a quorum of
`⌊2n/3⌋ + 1`:

- prices keep flowing while up to `n − quorum` publishers are down (1 of 4, 2 of 7, 2 of 9);
- a wrong or dishonest price needs a quorum of publishers to sign it;
- each publisher computes independently from its own exchange connections, so one bad connection
  doesn't produce a bad price.

## Accountability

- **Every signature is public and attributable.** The update names which publishers signed.
- **Equivocation is detectable.** Two different signed updates for one tick are kept by mirrors as
  evidence at `/v1/equivocations`.
- **Every price is traceable** to the committee config (exchanges and settings) that produced it,
  through the config hash in each update.
- **Keys can be removed** by a quorum through a committee rotation.
