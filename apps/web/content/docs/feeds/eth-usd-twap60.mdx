## About this feed

- **Settlement price.** The average of ETH/USD's finalized one-second prices over 60 s, signed once every 60 s. Moving it means holding the market off for the whole window, so settle on it rather than on a single second.
- It is published only at boundary ticks: the update whose publish time is a multiple of 60 s. To settle "at 12:05", read the update with publish time 12:05:00.
- The update at boundary `t` averages the finalized [ETH/USD](/docs/feeds/eth-usd) prices from `t − 61 s` to `t − 2 s`, each second weighted equally. It is about 2 s older than its timestamp.
- If fewer than 45 of those seconds have a price, that boundary has no TWAP: treat it as VOID. A leader can also leave a boundary without a TWAP; contracts should define what a missing boundary means (for example, use the next one).
- conf is the larger of the window's average conf and how much the price moved within it.
