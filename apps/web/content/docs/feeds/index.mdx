## How feeds are named

A symbol is `Crypto.<BASE>/<QUOTE>`, for example `Crypto.BTC/USDT`: the price of one BTC in USDT.
Its **feed ID** is `ckb_hash("LEAN/FEED/V1" || symbol)`. The SDK accepts either anywhere a feed is
expected. IDs are permanent: a feed's ID never changes, even when its exchanges or settings do.

## Native pairs: USD, USDT and USDC are different feeds

Every feed is priced only from markets that trade **exactly** that pair. BTC/USD comes from Coinbase,
Kraken and Bitstamp; BTC/USDT from seven USDT markets; BTC/USDC from eight USDC markets. Publishers
never convert one currency to another.

This matters when a stablecoin loses its peg: BTC/USDT then really shows BTC's price in USDT, and
USDT/USD shows the depeg. To get a USD price from a USDT feed, multiply by
[USDT/USD](/docs/feeds/usdt-usd) yourself (see [Derive cross rates](/docs/build/guides/cross-rates)).

## Categories

| Category | Feeds | What to expect |
|---|---|---|
| Deep liquidity | BTC, ETH, SOL in /USD, /USDT, /USDC | Many large exchanges, tight confidence, one-second updates |
| Stablecoin rate | USDT/USD | Stays near 1.0; use it for conversion and depeg checks |
| Thinner liquidity | CKB/USDT, CKB/USDC | Fewer exchanges and less depth; wider confidence, two-second updates, use conservative bounds |

## Market hours

Crypto trades around the clock, so every feed updates 24/7. A feed can still miss individual ticks
if too few of its exchanges are healthy at that moment; the update for that tick then leaves the
feed out. Your contract should never assume every tick has a price.

## Choosing a feed

1. Prefer the pair your application actually settles in. A USDT-settled market should use a /USDT
   feed, not a /USD feed.
2. Prefer feeds with more exchanges and tighter confidence for anything that liquidates or settles
   large amounts.
3. For CKB pairs, check the confidence before acting, and consider using the EMA for slow-moving
   decisions such as collateral ratios.
4. Read the [feed policy](/docs/feeds/policy) for how feeds are added, changed and retired.

The list of feeds also comes from the mirror: `GET /v1/feeds`.
